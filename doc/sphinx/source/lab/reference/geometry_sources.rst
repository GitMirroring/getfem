Geometry sources
================

-----------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

     - Value
   * - Name
     - ``geometries.0.name``
     - name

The curve is the polygon below, drawn and edited in the view as the polygons of a 2D geometry (vertices, fillets, curved sides, constraints, dimensions), open or closed. The ends of an open curve are the built-in selections start and end.

Built-in selections: ``all``, ``boundary``, ``start``, ``end``.

.. _lab-ref-source-surface:

surface: surface in 3D (flat domain + height)
---------------------------------------------

Meshers: ``getfem``, ``gmsh``, ``netgen``.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 20 60

   * - Field
     - Key
     - Value
   * - Name
     - ``geometries.0.name``
     - name
   * - Map
     - ``parametric``
     - one of ``false`` (a plane and a height), ``true`` (parametric (x, y, z of u, v))
   * - Origin
     - ``origin``
     - numbers or expressions of parameters, one per coordinate, or a vector parameter
   * - Axis x
     - ``axes.0``
     - numbers or expressions of parameters, one per coordinate, or a vector parameter
   * - Axis y
     - ``axes.1``
     - numbers or expressions of parameters, one per coordinate, or a vector parameter
   * - Height
     - ``height``
     - number or GWFL expression. Height along the normal: a number or an expression of the local coordinates x, y and the parameters ("0.2\*sqr(x)").

The flat domain (2D shape below) is drawn and its selections are defined in the local coordinates (x, y).

Built-in selections: ``all``, ``boundary``.

.. _lab-ref-source-swept_surface:

swept_surface: surface in 3D swept by a curve
---------------------------------------------

Meshers: ``getfem``.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 20 60

   * - Field
     - Key
     - Value
   * - Name
     - ``geometries.0.name``
     - name
   * - Sweep
     - ``sweep``
     - one of ``extrusion`` (extrusion along z (curve in the (x, y) plane)), ``revolution`` (revolution about z (curve in the (r, z) plane))
   * - From angle to angle (degrees)
     - ``angle``
     - numbers or expressions of parameters, one per coordinate, or a vector parameter

The swept curve is the polygon below, drawn and edited in the view as the polygons of a 2D geometry, open or closed, in the plane (r, z): it must not touch the axis r = 0.

Built-in selections: ``all``, ``boundary``.

.. _lab-ref-source-boundary:

boundary: boundary of a 3D geometry
-----------------------------------

Meshers: those of its solid.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 20 60

   * - Field
     - Key
     - Value
   * - Name
     - ``geometries.0.name``
     - name
   * - Of the geometry
     - ``geometry``
     - a name
   * - Faces
     - ``selection``
     - one of ``null`` (all (its whole boundary)). A boundary selection of the geometry: its faces only (an open surface)

A surface: the 3D geometry is meshed with the parameters of the mesh, then its faces are taken. Its boundary selections are built-in selections (parts) of the surface; the selection 'boundary' is the edge of an open surface.

Built-in selections: ``all``, ``boundary``.
