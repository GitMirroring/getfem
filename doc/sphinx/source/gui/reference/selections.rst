.. _gui-ref-selections:

Selection criteria
==================

A selection of a geometry is a boundary or a domain selection, defined by a criterion evaluated on the mesh. The form of a selection shows its name, its kind and the fields of its criterion; the key is the one of its ``criteria`` object.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Criterion
     - Kinds
     - Geometries
   * - ``plane``
     - boundary
     - any
   * - ``normal``
     - boundary
     - any
   * - ``in_box``
     - boundary, domain
     - any
   * - ``in_ball``
     - boundary
     - any
   * - ``physical``
     - boundary, domain
     - any
   * - ``union``
     - boundary, domain
     - any
   * - ``edges``
     - boundary
     - 2D box or csg
   * - ``faces``
     - boundary
     - 3D box or csg
   * - ``regions``
     - domain
     - 2D box or csg
