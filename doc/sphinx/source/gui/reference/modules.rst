.. _gui-ref-modules:

Modules
========

A module is a predefined physics acting on variables of its model. Its properties are given per domain selection, by a value or a material; its conditions apply on boundary selections; its macros can be used in every expression of the model.

Generic conditions
------------------

Conditions on any variable of a model, besides those of the modules:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Condition
     - Values
     - Fixes the solution
   * - ``dirichlet``
     - ``value`` (of the kind of the variable)
     - yes
   * - ``normal_dirichlet``
     - ``value`` (scalar)
     - yes
   * - ``neumann``
     - ``value`` (of the kind of the variable)
     -
   * - ``robin``
     - ``h`` (scalar), ``value`` (of the kind of the variable)
     - yes
