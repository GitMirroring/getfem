Transient global solver
-----------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 20 60

   * - Field
     - Key
     - Value
   * - Name
     - ``name``
     - name
   * - Type
     - ``type``
     - one of ``sequence``, ``staggered`` (staggered iterations), ``transient`` (transient (in time))
   * - Models, in order
     - ``steps``
     - list of names
   * - Start time
     - ``time.start``
     - number or GWFL expression
   * - End time
     - ``time.end``
     - number or GWFL expression
   * - Time step
     - ``time.step``
     - number or GWFL expression
   * - Save every (steps)
     - ``save.every``
     - integer
   * - Coupling at each step
     - ``coupling``
     - one of ``sequence`` (the models in turn), ``staggered`` (staggered iterations)
   * - Scheme of T (heat)
     - ``schemes.heat/T.type``
     - one of ``stationary`` (stationary (no time derivative: quasi-static)), ``theta`` (theta-method (1: implicit Euler, 0.5: Crank-Nicolson)), ``bdf2`` (BDF2 (second order; its first step: Crank-Nicolson)). The time scheme of the variable: stationary (no time derivative, the equation solved at each time), or a scheme of the order of its time derivative (theta-method, BDF2)
   * - Theta of T
     - ``schemes.heat/T.theta``
     - number or expression of parameters. The theta-method: 1, implicit Euler (robust, first order); 0.5, Crank-Nicolson (second order, may oscillate) (]0, 1])
   * - Scheme of S (other)
     - ``schemes.other/S.type``
     - one of ``stationary`` (stationary (no time derivative: quasi-static)), ``theta`` (theta-method (1: implicit Euler, 0.5: Crank-Nicolson)), ``bdf2`` (BDF2 (second order; its first step: Crank-Nicolson)). The time scheme of the variable: stationary (no time derivative, the equation solved at each time), or a scheme of the order of its time derivative (theta-method, BDF2)

At each time step, the models are solved in turn (or by staggered iterations), every equation written at the new time; the scheme of each variable in time gives its time derivative (Dot\_T for T) from its value and the previous ones; the time t may appear in the expressions. The variables start from their initial value; the results are saved every few steps (a VTU file each, a .pvd collection).
